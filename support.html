Support Us

Klez Fest Midwest is a community-powered festival, and your support helps make it possible. Generous financial assistance from our supporters has enabled us to present concerts like this year’s showcase by The Klezmatics in a world class concert hall.

Your tax-deductible donation helps us bring inspiring klezmer musicians to Madison, pay artists fairly, create opportunities for people to hear and experience this music, and build a festival that can grow from year to year. KFMW’s goal is to continue to present and celebrate this living and evolving tradition. Every dollar we receive pays for artist fees, travel, venues, and the many behind-the-scenes costs of putting on a festival.

Every contribution makes a difference. Whether you give $25, $50, $100, or whatever amount feels right, you’re helping us build a home for klezmer music in Madison. Thank you for helping Klez Fest Midwest thrive and grow!

You can contribute at supportuw.org/giveto/KlezFest26, which takes you directly to KFMW’s fund at the UW Foundation. When you reach the donation page, leave empty the “Find Your Cause” box. You are automatically registered as a KFMW donor when you follow this link—simply fill in the amount of your contribution.

Please note: The UW Foundation is an independent, tax-exempt corporation that raises, invests, and distributes funds for the benefit of UW–Madison. Contributions to the Foundation qualify for tax deduction to the extent allowed by law. The Foundation is registered for charitable solicitation in all states in which registration is required. Any required solicitation disclosures can be found at supportuw.org/disclosures. Questions about giving? Call 608-263-4545.
