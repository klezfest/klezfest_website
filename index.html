About

Klez Fest Midwest (KFMW), founded in 2024, is celebrating its third season this year. Directed by Meg Lamm, Dan Grabois, and Michael Bell, the festival brings international klezmer bands and local talent to Madison to explore this vibrant art form. KFMW welcomes traditional klezmer and klezmer blended with any other music styles, including blues, folk, Gypsy fire punk, Eastern European, and Latin music. KFMW events this year are free thanks to the support of our sponsors.
